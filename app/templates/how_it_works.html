How Community Parking Works

A collaborative platform designed to unlock unused private and community parking capacity while reducing street traffic and illegal roadside parking.

For Drivers

Find, reserve, and park seamlessly

1 Search & Filter

Search by destination locality, radius, price, or vehicle type. Toggle Emergency Mode for urgent immediate parking.

2 Smart Availability Check

View intelligent demand predictions (Likely Available, Occupied Soon, High Demand Area) and guaranteed open slot checks.

3 Book & Receive QR Ticket

Confirm date, time, and vehicle number. Once approved, your encrypted QR code ticket is generated.

4 Navigate & Self-Service Check-In

Launch 1-click Google Maps GPS navigation, scan the space's wall-mounted QR sign with your phone camera to check in, and rate on departure.

For Property Owners

Monetize unused driveways & garages

1 Register & Verify

Create an owner account and submit identity/address details for admin verification to ensure community safety.

2 List Parking Spaces

Specify address, GPS coordinates, vehicle types, available slots, operating hours, amenities, and hourly pricing.

3 Approve Bookings

Review incoming reservation requests with full visibility into driver name, vehicle plate, and schedule.

4 Print Wall Sign & Track Revenue

Print your space's unique wall-mounted QR signboard for autonomous driver check-ins, and track real-time revenue on your dashboard.
